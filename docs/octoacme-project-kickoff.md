# OctoAcme — Project Kickoff

## Purpose
Bridge project initiation (approval) and active planning/execution by aligning the delivery team and stakeholders on objectives, scope, timeline, and ways of working.

## When to Hold a Kickoff
Hold the kickoff after the project passes the initiation decision gate and the following are true:
- The Project One-pager is approved
- Success metrics are clear
- Stakeholder alignment is confirmed
- Team availability is secured

## Key Participants
- Project Manager (PM): facilitates the kickoff and captures decisions
- Product Manager (PdM): presents the problem statement, objectives, and success metrics
- Developers: represent the delivery team and implementation perspective
- QA/Testing: covers quality assurance and validation planning
- Stakeholders: sponsors and dependent teams who need alignment on outcomes and timing

## Kickoff Meeting Agenda (60–90 minutes)
1. Welcome & Introductions (5 min)
   - Confirm attendees, roles, and meeting goals
2. Project Overview (15 min)
   - Review the Project One-pager, problem statement, objectives, and success metrics
3. Scope & Timeline (15 min)
   - Review milestones, dependencies, and what is in or out of scope
4. Team & Ways of Working (15 min)
   - Confirm roles, communication cadence, and tools
5. Risks & Dependencies (10 min)
   - Review the initial risk register, cross-team dependencies, and escalation paths
6. Next Steps (10 min)
   - Confirm immediate actions, planning schedule, and open Q&A

## Deliverables from Kickoff
- Kickoff meeting held
- Shared understanding of objectives
- Project board skeleton created
- Communication plan confirmed
- Initial backlog items identified
- Risk register initialized
- Sprint planning scheduled

## Post-Kickoff Actions
- PM documents decisions and action items
- PM and PdM create the initial backlog
- Team sets up repo structure

## Success Criteria
- Team understands the "why" behind the project
- Roles are clear
- Dependencies are surfaced early
- Team is ready for planning
